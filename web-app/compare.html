BILLI

One live record everyone is inside

  • Something happens — four actions fire at once

Straight Answers

What Billi Doesn’t Have

We are the new name on this page. The fastest way to be worth trusting is to tell you where the older names are still ahead of us.

📞 No monitoring centre

Nobody sits watching a screen on your behalf. Billi coordinates the people you chose. That is a deliberate design — the neighbour with your spare key beats a call centre in the first two minutes — but if you want staffed 24/7 monitoring, a medical-alert service does that and we do not.

🚓 No live 911 dispatch integration

Billi builds a 911-ready packet — identity, location, medical protocol, evidence, full timeline — but a human still makes the call and reads it out. Life360 Gold dispatches ambulances directly. We do not, yet.

📱 iPhone text alerts are limited

Apple permits no third-party app to send a text without a person tapping send. Real SMS works on Android through the phone’s own SIM. A carrier gateway for iPhone is built and pending a paid account. If everyone you would protect is on iPhone, wait for us.

⌚ Wearables aren’t live hardware yet

Watches, BLE tags and smart glasses appear in the interface; your phone is the real sensor today. AngelSense ships dedicated hardware a child cannot leave behind, and for elopement specifically that is a real advantage over any phone app.
